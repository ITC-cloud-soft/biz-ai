# Marketing deck source — Bizアシスタント (Biz Assistant Framework) for executives

Customer-facing brand: **Bizアシスタント**. The technical name of the framework (Biz Assistant Framework, packages `@dsh-bridge/*`) appears only on the appendix slide for the IT team.

`../biz-assistant-for-executives.pptx` (and `.pdf`) is a 28-slide Japanese deck for owners and executives of small and mid-sized
companies (27 slides + 1 appendix for the IT team), with speaker notes on every slide. It is generated by `build.cjs`
(pptxgenjs); do not edit the .pptx by hand — change `build.cjs` and rebuild.

Storyline (2026-10-29): Bizアシスタント is a **truly general-purpose** framework that turns **any IT system** into an AI agent / AI
assistant — AI 社員 in every employee's own chat (a change of how work is done: from people opening screens,
entering, checking and chasing, to the AI 社員 watching, notifying, asking the right person and drafting while people decide in
chat). The system adds the bridge — the items on slide 18 (the same list, precisely, is `docs/GUIDELINE.md`「既存の業務システムを
つなぐ：チェックリスト」) — and Bizアシスタント provides the rest (slide 6). Each person receives only what concerns them, and the
business system decides who that is (slide 15). A new system can be built the same way. ケアビリング is the proof (implemented,
running in the demo environment).

2026-10-30: the AI 社員 has the **name and personality the company sets** (呼び名「さくら」, tone, company introduction,
personality; always says it is an AI; the rules do not change — slide 12, section 「AI 社員の人柄」), **answers how-to questions**
from the system's manual with a link to the screen the person may open, and **remembers** each person's preferences
(personal data is not kept; the sixth job 「覚えておく」 on slide 5 says the same — slide 13). Slide 1 leads with
「どの IT システムも、AI エージェント（AI 社員・AI アシスタント）に。」; slide 6 lists the name / personality settings and the
memory among what Bizアシスタント provides; the proof (slide 20) and the IT appendix (slide 23: 8 plugins) follow. The 30-minute
estimate (slide 17) is unchanged.

2026-11-01: new section 「自分で育つシステム」 (slides 20–23): the second AI, **運用 AI** (Ops Agent), watches, rolls back,
fixes, tests and updates the system itself and talks only to the administrator (slide 20); it runs **on another server**, so it keeps
working and keeps the last records when the business system stops (slide 21); what it may do alone vs. what people decide — money,
permissions, personal data, external connections, its own rules — and the five steps of autonomy (slide 22); **security updates every
night** and response to attacks, own systems only (slide 23). Design: framework `docs/RSI_SELF_IMPROVING_SYSTEM.md`.

2026-11-01 (RSI design v0.3): the section is rewritten to **"development environment: the AI fixes and releases by itself;
production: checked first in a verification environment, then the person in charge decides in chat"** — now 5 slides (20–24).
Slide 20: the six jobs of 運用 AI (見張る・調べる・直して試す・提案する・備える・覚える). Slide 21 (new): **the two environments as a
simple diagram** — 開発環境 (見つける → 直す → 試す → そのまま反映 → 覚える) and 本番環境 (見つける → 検証環境で再現 → 直す → 検証環境で
確かめる → チャットで提案 → 担当者が判断); the 検証環境 is the same as production except for its size, with personal data hidden.
Slide 22: the separate server; rolling back production is proposed in chat and done after a one-tap approval. Slide 23: what the AI
does alone (development environment only) vs. what the person in charge decides in chat (every production change: updates,
restarts / rollbacks, security measures, the AI's own rules); there is no step where the AI updates production alone. Slide 24:
security — nightly checks, updates verified in the 検証環境, and every security measure in production is approved in chat first.
The proof, adoption, call to action and IT appendix moved to slides 25–28.

Same visual family as the technical deck `../../biz-assistant-framework.pptx`: theme "DSH Bridge Ink" (dark teal / saffron),
Yu Gothic UI headings, Meiryo UI body. The motif is one kanji in a coloured circle; the "一日" slides show phone mock-ups in
the AI 秘書 message style of `care-ai-native/docs/design/IM_MESSAGE_STYLE.md`.

## Rebuild

Requirements: Node 22+, `pptxgenjs` 4.x (tested with 4.0.1), and for PDF / PNG Windows with PowerPoint.

```powershell
# pptxgenjs must be resolvable from build.cjs; if it is installed elsewhere, point NODE_PATH at that node_modules
$env:NODE_PATH = 'C:\path\to\node_modules'      # e.g. after: npm install --prefix C:\path\to pptxgenjs@4.0.1
node build.cjs                                   # writes ..\biz-assistant-for-executives.pptx

# PDF (and optional PNG per slide, for checking) through PowerPoint
.\render.ps1 -Pptx (Resolve-Path ..\biz-assistant-for-executives.pptx) `
             -Pdf  "$PWD\..\biz-assistant-for-executives.pdf" `
             -PngDir "$env:TEMP\dsh-exec-png"
```

`render.ps1` opens the deck read-only; if PowerPoint was not running before, it quits it afterwards. Paths must be absolute.

After a change, look at every PNG for text overflow, odd line breaks and overlaps. Japanese line breaking (kinsoku) depends on
the text being tagged `ja-JP`: the helper `T()` and the title calls set `lang: 'ja-JP'`; keep it on any new text.

## Content rules

- Audience: non-technical 経営者・事業責任者. No technical terms on slides 1–27 (API / manifest etc. only on the appendix).
- 運用 AI: never say or imply that the AI changes production by itself. In production, updates, restarts / rollbacks and security
  measures are decided by the person in charge in chat; only the development environment is automatic.
- The message: any IT system can work with AI 社員 by adding the bridge (slide 18); Bizアシスタント provides everything else.
- The AI 社員 always says it is an AI (signature 「— さくら（… AI 秘書）」); never present it as a person.
  Do not claim that the current system stays exactly as it is or needs no change (「今のシステムはそのまま」) — the bridge is real
  work, sized in the first diagnosis. Building a new system on the same framework is an option, not the headline.
- Keep claims factual: what the framework and ケアビリング actually do (e.g. recipients decided by the business system, group chats
  denied, own chats linked by each employee).
- Natural business Japanese; no Chinese words (平台・免打扰・插件・会话・推送 …), little katakana jargon.
- Names, dates, counts and messages are fictional. No real people, no customer data.
- No prices, no competitor names, no promises (「必ず」など). Numbers that are not facts are labelled as 試算例 / 目安.

## Contact line

The お問い合わせ line on the last slide comes from `contact.local.json` in this folder when it exists (`{ "contact": "［会社名］　［ご担当者名］　［メールアドレス・電話番号］" }`).
The file is gitignored: the committed deck keeps the placeholder, and each company builds its own copy with its contact.

## Bizアシスタント：記録するシステムを、考えて動くシステムに（図解中心の営業資料、2026-11-13）

`../biz-assistant-features.pptx` / `.pdf`（21 枚）と精簡版 `../biz-assistant-features-short.pptx` / `.pdf`（8 枚）は `build-features.cjs` が生成する（日本語）。
上の `biz-assistant-for-executives` は残したまま、別の資料として作った。

- メッセージ：登録・照会・更新といった記録と決められた業務処理が中心の IT システムを、**考える・覚える・自分から動く（職責を自分で果たす）**システムに変える。決めるのはいつも人。運用 AI でシステムそのものも自分で育つ。
- 見た目は maintenance-ai の運用 AI 営業資料（`docs/slides/ops-ai/src/build-features.cjs`）と同じ：テーマ DSH Bridge Ink、Meiryo、react-icons のバッジ、流れ図、短いラベル。説明はすべてスピーカーノート。
- 筋立て（長い版）：表紙 → これまでとこれから → 悩み → 3 つの力（考える・覚える・自分から動く）→ AI 社員のいる一日（定時の報告・至急・承認・AI の提案・名前と人柄・届け先）→ しくみ（全体像・業種を選ばない・ブリッジ・安心の 6 つ）→ 運用 AI → 試算例・実績・導入 → まとめ。
- 精簡版：表紙・これまでとこれから・3 つの力・一日・どのシステムにも・安心・自分で育つ・まとめ。
- チャットの画面は図で描いた架空の内容。試算例（30 名 × 30 分 × 20 日 = 300 時間）は「試算例（前提を置いた参考値）」の表示を残す。実績の数字（チャット 6 種類・自動チェック 52 項目・部品 9 つ・運用 AI の 2 分）は 2026-11 時点の事実。お問い合わせ先は載せない。
